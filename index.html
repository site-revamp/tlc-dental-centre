More patient FAQs
How do I request an appointment?

Call +65 6338 6639 or message the clinic on WhatsApp. Share your reason for visiting and preferred timing. A WhatsApp message is an appointment request; wait for the team's confirmation.

Can I ask about braces or Invisalign?

Yes. TLC offers orthodontic care, including braces and Invisalign. Dr Enrica Sham is an MOH Accredited Specialist in Orthodontics. An assessment is needed to determine suitable options.

Where is TLC Dental Centre?

The clinic is at 360 Orchard Road, #05-05, International Building, Singapore 238869. Contact the clinic to confirm opening hours and appointment availability.

Where can I check payment options?

Visit TLC's financing information for details about CHAS, MediSave, CDA and instalment options. Eligibility and applicable treatments vary; confirm the details with the clinic.
