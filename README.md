# TLC Dental Centre — Homepage Redesign

## Website Overview

TLC Dental Centre is a dental practice on Orchard Road, Singapore, providing general dental care, specialist orthodontics, and restorative and cosmetic treatments. Its website helps patients explore treatment options and contact the clinic about appointments.

The redesigned homepage improves appointment button readability, clarifies the WhatsApp enquiry process and provides direct access to treatment information.

## Three Main Improvements

### 1. Higher-Contrast Appointment Buttons

Dark navy text replaces white text on the existing pink appointment buttons. This preserves the pink colour while improving label contrast and readability.

### 2. Clear WhatsApp Appointment Labels

Buttons clearly identify WhatsApp as their destination and explain that appointments require confirmation from the clinic team. Previously, “Book an Appointment” opened WhatsApp without indicating this beforehand. The revised wording sets clearer expectations before patients make contact.

### 3. Direct Treatment Links

Six direct treatment links appear in three columns on desktop and two on mobile. These reduce reliance on the previous dropdown, which required selecting a treatment and pressing “Go”, and broaden discovery beyond the limited featured-service selection.

## Design Improvements

- **Colour and readability:** Dark navy labels improve contrast while retaining the pink appointment buttons.
- **Interaction consistency:** WhatsApp labels match their destination and explain the confirmation step.
- **Responsive layout:** Treatment links use three desktop columns and two mobile columns.
- **User experience:** Direct links remove an extra step when opening treatment information.
